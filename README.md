# Botonista

Futebol de botão na mesa. Pick a nation and a formation, then flick your buttons with the palheta through a 16-team World Cup knockout against the CPU. First to 3 goals wins each match.

Portuguese (BR) by default, with English in the settings.

## Play on iPhone

1. Open the game's GitHub Pages address in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open Botonista from the home screen. It runs full screen and works offline after the first visit.

## Account and sync

Botonista uses the same Orchid-42 account as the other Orchid-42 games (email and password, or Google). Signing in saves your settings, unlocked palhetas, stats and the current Copa online and syncs them across devices. Without an account, progress stays on the device.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole game
- `sw.js`: offline cache
- `manifest.webmanifest`, `icons/`: home-screen app setup
- `privacy.html`: privacy policy
